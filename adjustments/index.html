The basics

What Is a Chiropractic Adjustment?

A precise, controlled force applied to a spinal joint to restore proper alignment, reduce nerve irritation and improve mobility.

A chiropractic adjustment, also called spinal manipulation, involves applying a controlled, high-velocity, low-amplitude thrust to a specific vertebral joint. The goal is to correct joint dysfunctions in the spine that compress nerves, restrict movement and cause pain.

  • The characteristic "pop" or "crack" you may hear is simply the release of nitrogen gas from the joint capsule, similar to cracking your knuckles
  • It is not bone grinding on bone
  • Adjustments are safe, effective and drug-free

After a car accident

Why Adjustments Work After a Car Accident

The sudden forces of a collision routinely knock vertebrae out of their normal alignment. Even a low-speed rear-end impact generates enough force to misalign the cervical spine. Left untreated, these joint dysfunctions cause:

  • 01

    Chronic neck and back pain

  • 02

    Nerve compression causing arm or leg numbness

  • 03

    Headaches and migraines

  • 04

    Reduced range of motion

  • 05

    Accelerated disc degeneration

Chiropractic adjustments directly address the source of these problems by restoring proper joint position, relieving nerve pressure and allowing your body to heal the way it was designed to.

Benefits

Benefits of Chiropractic Adjustments After an Accident

Immediate Pain Relief

Adjustments reduce pressure on irritated nerves and relax surrounding muscles, often providing noticeable relief within the first few visits.

Restored Range of Motion

Accident injuries cause inflammation and muscle guarding that restrict movement. Adjustments restore joint mobility so you can move freely again.

Reduced Inflammation

Spinal manipulation triggers the release of anti-inflammatory compounds, helping your body manage post-accident swelling without relying on medication.

Prevention of Chronic Pain

Untreated spinal joint dysfunctions often become permanent sources of chronic pain. Early adjustment care stops this from happening.

Non-Invasive and Drug-Free

No injections, no surgery, no opioids. Chiropractic care is one of the safest and most effective ways to treat accident injuries.

Documented Medical Record

Every visit produces clinical documentation that supports your personal injury claim and demonstrates the impact of your injuries.

Your visit

What to Expect at Your Visit

  1. Evaluation and Spinal Assessment

    We perform a comprehensive exam including range of motion testing, orthopedic and neurological assessments, and postural analysis to identify all joint dysfunctions.

  2. Imaging If Needed

    We order X-rays or refer for MRI when indicated to rule out fractures and identify disc involvement before beginning hands-on care.

  3. Targeted Adjustments

    We apply gentle, precise adjustments to affected spinal segments, commonly the cervical (neck) and lumbar (lower back) regions after auto accidents.

  4. Adjunct Therapies

    Adjustments are often combined with soft tissue work, IASTM or dry needling for a comprehensive treatment approach tailored to your injury pattern.

  5. Home Care and Progress Tracking

    We guide you through exercises and lifestyle adjustments between visits, and track your progress with regular reassessments throughout your care plan.

Are chiropractic adjustments safe after a car accident?
Yes, for the vast majority of patients. Before any adjustment, we conduct a thorough evaluation and imaging review to identify any contraindications such as fractures. Adjustments are then modified or avoided in specific areas as needed. Serious adverse events from chiropractic adjustments are extremely rare.
How many adjustments will I need?
It depends on the severity of your injuries. Many patients notice improvement within the first few visits. We reassess your progress regularly and adjust the treatment plan accordingly.
Will my auto insurance or MedPay cover adjustments?
In most cases, yes. Chiropractic care is covered under auto liability insurance, MedPay and most health insurance plans. Read more about MedPay coverage in Lafayette.
Can I get adjusted if I'm sore right after the accident?
Yes. We adapt the technique to your current state. Gentler, low-force methods are often used in the acute phase immediately after an injury. Care is never forced past your comfort level.
