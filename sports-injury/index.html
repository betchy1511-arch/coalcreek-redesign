For athletes

Chiropractic Care Built for Athletes

Whether you're training for your first 5K, competing on the weekend or playing recreational sports after work, your body takes a beating. Coal Creek Chiropractic is built to help athletes at every level recover faster, move better and stay in the game longer.

Dr. Johnston holds the Certified Chiropractic Sports Physician (CCSP) credential, one of the highest post-graduate sports medicine designations available to chiropractors, as well as TPI (Titleist Performance Institute) certification. This combination gives him a uniquely deep understanding of how the body moves under athletic demand and how to identify the root cause of sports-related pain and dysfunction.

What we treat

Common Conditions We Treat

Runner's Knee and IT Band

Hip and lower extremity biomechanics drive most running injuries. We identify and correct the movement faults causing your pain, not just treat the symptom.

Shoulder Injuries

Rotator cuff strain, AC joint sprains and shoulder impingement respond well to chiropractic adjustments and soft tissue therapy.

Golf and Racquet Sports Back Pain

Rotational sports demand thoracic mobility and core stability. We focus on the biomechanics behind these movements and the injuries they cause.

Ankle and Knee Sprains

Joint manipulation and rehabilitation of the lower extremity restores proper mechanics and reduces re-injury risk after ligament sprains.

Hip Flexor and Hamstring Strains

Soft tissue therapy including IASTM and dry needling accelerates healing of muscle strains and breaks down scar tissue from chronic overuse.

Neck and Upper Back Pain

Cyclists, swimmers and overhead athletes frequently develop cervical and thoracic tension. Targeted adjustments and mobility work provide lasting relief.

TPI

Titleist Performance Institute (TPI) Certified

TPI is the world's leading golf fitness and performance education organization. TPI-certified practitioners are trained to screen athletes for the physical limitations that cause swing faults, pain and injury. Dr. Johnston uses TPI's systematic movement assessment to identify restrictions in the hips, thoracic spine and shoulders that create both performance loss and injury risk, for golfers and all rotational athletes.

The TPI screen evaluates mobility, stability and motor control throughout the entire kinetic chain. For golfers specifically, this means identifying exactly which physical limitation is causing your swing fault, whether it's limited hip internal rotation, poor thoracic mobility or inadequate lead-side stability, and treating the root cause rather than the symptom.

Non-golfers benefit equally from TPI's movement framework. The rotational demands of the golf swing are shared by tennis, baseball, hockey and virtually any sport requiring power generation through the trunk.

Weekend Warrior? We've Got You

You may not train every day, but when you play, you go all in. Weekend warriors are actually at higher injury risk than regular athletes, because the demands of game day exceed what the body has been prepared for during the week.

  • 03

Can I see Dr. Johnston even if I'm not in pain?
Absolutely. Many athletes come in for maintenance care and performance optimization between seasons, to stay mobile, address imbalances and prevent the injuries that sideline them.
How is sports chiropractic different from regular chiropractic?
Sports chiropractic integrates biomechanical analysis, functional movement screening and sports-specific rehabilitation alongside traditional adjustments. The goal isn't just pain relief. It's performance and resilience.
Do I need a referral?
No referral needed. Call or book online and we'll get you in quickly, same-day when available.
