Your care plan

What Your Treatment Looks Like, Step by Step

Every patient who comes to Coal Creek after an auto accident receives individualized care, but the process follows a clear, structured path designed to get you better as efficiently as possible.

Book Your Evaluation

  1. Comprehensive Initial Evaluation

    A thorough assessment of your spine, joints and soft tissues to identify all injuries, including those not yet causing pain.

  2. Personalized Treatment Plan

    Based on your injuries, a structured care plan with clear goals, timelines and measurable outcomes.

  3. Spinal Adjustments

    Targeted manipulation to restore proper spinal alignment, reduce nerve irritation and relieve pain caused by the impact.

  4. Soft Tissue Therapy

    Massage, myofascial release and manual therapy techniques to reduce muscle spasm, inflammation and scar tissue formation.

  5. Rehabilitation Exercises

    Guided exercises to rebuild strength, restore range of motion and stabilize injured areas as you recover.

  6. Ongoing Documentation

    Detailed records of your injuries, treatment progress and functional improvements throughout your care, essential for your insurance claim or legal case.

  7. Insurance Coordination

    We work directly with your auto insurance provider and, if applicable, coordinate with your attorney so billing and records are handled correctly.

Important

Why Timing Matters

Seek care within 72 hours of your accident if at all possible. There are two important reasons for this:

  1. Early treatment produces better outcomes.Inflammation and muscle guarding set in quickly after an injury. The sooner chiropractic care begins, the more effectively we can reduce inflammation, restore proper alignment and prevent scar tissue from forming around injured tissues.
  2. Delayed care can affect your insurance claim.Insurance companies sometimes use gaps in treatment as evidence that your injuries weren't serious. A prompt initial evaluation creates a clear medical record tying your injuries to the accident.

I don't feel pain right after my accident. Do I still need to be seen?
Yes, this is very common. Adrenaline released during the accident can mask pain for 24 to 72 hours. Inflammation, muscle guarding and nerve symptoms often emerge days later. A gap in treatment, or no treatment at all, can also hurt your insurance claim even if you develop symptoms later. An early evaluation documents your condition from the start.
Should I see a medical doctor or a chiropractor first after a car accident?
If you have signs of a serious emergency, such as a head injury, loss of consciousness, severe chest pain or an obvious fracture, go to the ER first. For the soft tissue and spinal injuries that make up most car accident cases, a chiropractor focused on accident injuries is the right first call. Dr. Johnston does a full evaluation, orders imaging if needed and creates a documented treatment plan.
How long after an accident can I see a chiropractor?
You can see a chiropractor at any point, but sooner is better. Most personal injury attorneys and insurance professionals recommend being evaluated within 72 hours of the accident. Waiting weeks can make it harder to connect your injuries to the accident. If it has been a while, come in anyway. Documentation is better late than never.
Does it matter who was at fault in the accident?
No, you can receive chiropractic care regardless of fault. If you were not at fault, the at-fault driver's liability insurance typically covers your medical care. If you were at fault, your own MedPay coverage can help cover treatment. Read more about MedPay coverage in Lafayette.
Will chiropractic care affect my personal injury settlement?
Consistent chiropractic treatment typically strengthens your claim by creating a clear medical record linking your injuries to the accident and documenting the course and cost of your care. Detailed SOAP notes, functional assessments and narrative reports are available for your attorney.
